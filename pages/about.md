Hi! I’m **Yasmine Mustafa**, a computer scientist and AI researcher working at the intersection of
**machine learning**,
**multimodal AI**,
**medical imaging**, and
**trustworthy artificial intelligence**.

I recently completed my PhD in Computer Science at Missouri University of Science and Technology,
where my research focused on developing interpretable and anatomically informed AI systems for
neuroimaging and healthcare applications. My work combines ideas from
**deep learning**,
**computer vision**,
**transformers**, and
**multimodal representation learning**
to build models that are not only accurate, but also explainable, robust, and clinically meaningful.

My recent research explores:
- anatomically informed attention mechanisms,
- multimodal fusion of imaging and clinical data,
- explainable AI methods for neurodegenerative disease analysis,
- efficient 3D vision transformer pipelines,
- and foundation models for healthcare AI.

I am particularly interested in AI systems that can operate effectively under real-world conditions,
including incomplete, noisy, or heterogeneous data settings. Beyond performance, I care deeply about
interpretability, fairness, accessibility, and human-centered AI design.

In addition to research, I enjoy teaching, mentoring students, and designing engaging technical learning
environments. I value hands-on and project-based approaches to AI education that encourage critical
thinking, collaboration, and practical problem solving.
