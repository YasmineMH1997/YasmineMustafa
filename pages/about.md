Hi! I’m **Yasmine Mustafa**, a researcher working at the intersection of **deep learning**, **medical imaging**, and **trustworthy AI**. I build **multimodal models** to improve **disease disease** detection and interpretation from **medical data**. I care about methods that are not just accurate, but **explainable**, **robust**, and clinically meaningful. Recent work explores **anatomically informed attention**, **Jacobian-based saliency/feature maps**, and efficient 3D pipelines for neuroimaging.

---

## Research Interests
- Multimodal learning (e.g., MRI/PET + clinical text/tabular)
- Vision Transformers & Large Language Models
- Explainability & Trustworthiness
- Preprocessing + robust pipelines for neuroimaging
- Foundation models for medical imaging

## Highlights
