Hi! I'm **Yasmine Mustafa**, an **Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Actuarial Science, Data Science Program, at the American University in Cairo (AUC)**. I am a computer scientist and AI researcher working at the intersection of **machine learning**, **multimodal artificial intelligence**, **medical imaging**, and **trustworthy AI**.

I earned my **PhD in Computer Science from Missouri University of Science and Technology**, where my research focused on developing interpretable and anatomically informed AI systems for neuroimaging and healthcare applications. My work integrates **deep learning**, **computer vision**, **vision transformers**, and **multimodal representation learning** to develop AI models that are accurate, explainable, robust, and clinically meaningful.

My current research focuses on anatomically informed machine learning, multimodal fusion of neuroimaging and clinical data, explainable AI for neurodegenerative disease analysis, efficient 3D vision transformers, and foundation models for healthcare applications. I am particularly interested in developing AI systems that remain reliable under real-world challenges, including missing modalities, heterogeneous datasets, and limited labeled data.

Beyond healthcare AI, I am interested in the broader applications of **machine learning and data science**, particularly interdisciplinary research that connects computational methods with real-world scientific and societal challenges.

As an educator, I am passionate about **teaching, mentoring, and engaging students in research**. My teaching philosophy emphasizes hands-on learning, critical thinking, collaboration, and practical problem-solving. I strive to create learning environments where students can connect theoretical foundations with meaningful applications of data science and artificial intelligence.
