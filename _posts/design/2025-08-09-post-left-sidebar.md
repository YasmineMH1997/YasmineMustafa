I'm happy to share that I have received the **Best Paper Award 🎉** at the **Pacific-Asia Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (PAKDD) 2025** Workshop on *Pattern Mining and Machine Learning for Bioinformatics* for my recent work titled:

**"Unlocking Neural Transparency: Jacobian Maps for Explainable AI in Alzheimer's Detection."**

---

## 🧠 Jacobian Maps Make Alzheimer’s AI Models Trustworthy
Early detection of Alzheimer’s disease is critical—but while AI has become impressively accurate at diagnosis, its “black box” nature keeps it out of the clinic.  

🎯 Deep learning models can detect Alzheimer’s with high accuracy—but clinicians don’t trust what they can’t understand. Most AI tools lack transparency, and current explanation techniques like heatmaps often fall short, especially in medical imaging.

💡 This paper introduces **Jacobian Maps (JMs)** as a pre-model explainability tool that transforms brain images (MRI, CT, PET) into detailed maps of localized volume changes—**before** the AI model ever sees the data. By embedding anatomical meaning into the input, the model’s predictions become both accurate and clinically interpretable.  

**Results:** JMs achieved state-of-the-art performance:
- **95.2% accuracy** for classifying cognitively normal patients  
- **90.2% accuracy** for detecting mild, moderate, or severe dementia
